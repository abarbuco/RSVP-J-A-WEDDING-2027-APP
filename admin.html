Seating List & QR Codes

This list is the guest list — only names on it can RSVP. Upload your Excel seating chart (columns "Name" or "Full Name", "Table" or "Table Number", optionally "Nickname", and optionally "Guests" for how many each invitation allows), or add guests one at a time. Each guest gets a personal QR code that shows their table and the program when scanned — no typing needed. Anyone RSVPing can search and add any other names already on this list — their whole family, for instance — to their own RSVP in one go, with no limit, or just RSVP for themselves alone. "Guests allowed" is now just a headcount note for your own planning; it no longer caps anything on the RSVP form itself.

Tip: to give someone a plus-one without a separate "Guests" column, add a second row (or use "Add / Update Guest" again) with their name plus "+1" — e.g. "Kyle Josef" and then "Kyle Josef +1". This bumps Kyle's own Guests allowed to 2 and adds "Kyle Josef +1" as its own guest-list entry, so it can be typed in as his plus-one's name when he RSVPs.

Use this if you're confirming guests yourself (e.g. by phone) instead of waiting for them to RSVP on the site — each checked guest gets their own real "attending" RSVP under their own name, at 1 guest each (their own Guests Allowed still applies if they RSVP themselves later).
